Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how ICHA GLOBAL LIMITED handles information when you visit ICHA AI Studio at studio.tanphat.shop.

Information you provide

If you create an account, the registration form collects your name and email address and sends your password to our authentication provider to create your account. Do not use a password that you use for another important service.

Authentication and service providers

Account authentication and account records are handled using Appwrite. The application uses an authentication session to keep you signed in. We do not display your password in your public profile. Appwrite and its infrastructure may process account information to provide authentication and database services under their own service terms and privacy documentation.

Cookies and session information

When you sign in, the site sets a first-party session cookie named icha_session. It is configured as HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax and is used to maintain your authenticated session. The current application sets an expiry of up to 14 days. You can sign out to clear the session cookie.

How we use information

  • To create and maintain customer accounts.
  • To authenticate users and protect account access.
  • To respond to support requests and maintain the website.
  • To investigate errors, misuse or security incidents.

Payments and AI generation

Automated AI generation and online payment processing are not currently enabled in the studio. Do not send payment until an official payment process and order confirmation are provided. If these services are introduced, this policy will be updated before the relevant information is collected.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that operate the website and authentication infrastructure, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. Account information is retained while needed to provide the account and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of account information, subject to applicable law and security checks. Contact info@tanphat.shop.

Children

ICHA AI Studio is not designed for children under 13. Do not create an account if you are below the minimum age required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
