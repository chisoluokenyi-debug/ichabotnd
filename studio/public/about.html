About ICHA AI Studio

ICHA AI Studio is a creative technology project from ICHA GLOBAL LIMITED. It is being developed to help creators and small businesses plan and produce digital video and voice content.

What we are building

The project is focused on practical creative workflows: preparing a clear video idea, shaping a short script, planning scenes, and creating responsible voiceovers. Our aim is to make these workflows easier to understand and use on everyday devices, including mobile phones.

Current availability

The studio website and customer account interface are available. Automated video and voice generation are not currently enabled for customer use. We will only announce a feature as available after its safety, reliability, usage limits and support process have been tested.

Our approach

  • Explain what a feature does before asking a customer to use it.
  • Be clear about pricing, limits and service availability.
  • Respect privacy and obtain permission before using a person's voice or likeness.
  • Publish practical information that helps creators make informed decisions.
