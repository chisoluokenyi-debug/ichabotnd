IchaBot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IchaBot handles information when a person or business administrator uses IchaBot to connect and manage a Facebook Page. IchaBot provides tools for Page messaging, automated replies, comment responses and broadcasts.

Information we collect

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, IchaBot may process:

  • Facebook account information returned during sign-in, such as Facebook user ID, name and, when provided, email address.
  • Facebook Page information, including Page ID and Page name.
  • Page access tokens needed to provide the connected Page features. These tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Information delivered through Page webhooks, such as messaging events, sender identifiers and comment-related events, so that enabled automations can work.
  • Settings and content entered by the user, including welcome messages, default replies, comment replies and broadcast messages, along with broadcast scheduling and delivery status.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the service, protect sessions and prevent duplicate processing of webhook events.

How we use information

We use this information only as needed to authenticate users, connect the Facebook Pages they select, operate the requested automations, send permitted replies or broadcasts, save settings, maintain service security, troubleshoot errors and comply with legal requirements.

Facebook information and permissions

When you connect Facebook, Meta may share information with IchaBot according to the permissions you approve and Meta's platform terms. IchaBot uses Page access only to provide the features requested by the account user. IchaBot does not sell Facebook information or use it for advertising unrelated to the service.

Sharing

IchaBot does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by the hosting and database providers used to operate IchaBot, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. Those providers process information to support the service.

Storage and security

Information is stored in the application's database and hosting environment. Page access tokens are encrypted before being stored. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authenticated sessions and webhook verification. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain connected Page settings and records, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. When a user disconnects or asks for deletion, the app operator will review the request and delete or de-identify information that is no longer required, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your IchaBot account or connected Page, contact the operator through the Facebook Page or business channel through which you obtained IchaBot, and include the Facebook account or Page involved. Do not send passwords or access tokens. You can also remove IchaBot's access from your Facebook or Meta Business integrations.

Children

IchaBot is intended for business and Page administration, not for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates when this version was published. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the IchaBot operator through the Facebook Page or business channel through which you accessed IchaBot.
